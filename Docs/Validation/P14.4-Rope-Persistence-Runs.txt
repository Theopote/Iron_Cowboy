P14.4 rope persistence and on-foot control — 2026-09-14 — Unreal Engine 5.8.2

Human feedback trigger
  Sustained tension broke the rope too easily.
  A running wild horse should pull the mounted player before the rope gives way.
  Dismounted or fallen players should keep the rope and continue calming while following or being dragged.

Implementation
  Ordinary high tension transmits force and no longer starts a break timer.
  Break risk requires high separating speed plus sudden anchor-side deceleration.
  The lassoed horse continues struggling away and slows as control progress rises.
  Shock and lateral pull can force a rider fall.
  A fallen rider is dragged for up to 2 seconds, then stands in Pulled state without dropping the rope.
  On-foot tension accelerates the rider toward the horse while movement and Space control remain available.
  HUD distinguishes sustained pull, sudden jolt, downed dragging and on-foot pulling.
